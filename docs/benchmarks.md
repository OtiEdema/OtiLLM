# Benchmarks

The previous benchmark summary for OtiLLM v2.4 is deprecated on the JAIR publication branch.

The OtiLLM v2.0 JAIR evaluation is being rebuilt under `experiments/jair_v2_rebuild/`. Planned benchmark families include TruthfulQA, FEVER, HotpotQA and Natural Questions, together with a retrieval-stress suite. Exact benchmark versions, item manifests, evidence corpora and licences must be archived before publication results are reported.

No benchmark number should be cited from this file.
